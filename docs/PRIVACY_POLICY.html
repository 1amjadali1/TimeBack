Privacy Policy for TimeBack

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

TimeBack ("we", "our", or "the app") is a digital wellbeing and focus application designed to help users monitor and regulate their short-form video consumption (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight).

2. Use of Android AccessibilityService API

TimeBack uses Android's AccessibilityService API solely to:

  • Detect scroll events on vertical short-form video feeds within supported applications.
  • Increment the user's daily reel counter in real time.
  • Enforce user-configured digital wellbeing limits by closing the video viewport when the limit is reached.

What we DO NOT do:

  • We DO NOT read, capture, or store your private messages, chats, or emails.
  • We DO NOT record keystrokes, passwords, or personal credentials.
  • We DO NOT record audio, video, or camera streams.
  • We DO NOT sell or transfer accessibility data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

All reel counters, limits, and configuration preferences are stored locally on your device using Android encrypted DataStore. No video watching history or account data is transmitted to external servers without your explicit consent.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All subscription payments are handled securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not process or store your credit card, debit card, or UPI banking information directly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@timeback.app
