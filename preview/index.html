Engineering & Play Store Specs

1. Detection Mechanism

Uses ReelDetectionService : AccessibilityService. Hooks into TYPE_VIEW_SCROLLED on Instagram (com.instagram.android) and YouTube (com.google.android.youtube) with debouncing to prevent multi-count on a single gesture.

2. Floating Bubble Service

Uses FloatingOverlayService : Service with SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW. Displays the live counter and reactive mini-mascot directly over third-party apps.

3. Hard Limit Blocking Engine

When the user exceeds their daily quota, the service triggers GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK or fires an intent for BlockOverlayActivity with FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK, denying access to the reels viewer.

4. Google Play Policy Compliance

Crucial for approval: Includes an in-app Prominent Disclosure Dialog before requesting accessibility permission, strictly stating no personal data or message reading occurs.

5. Google Play Billing v7

Subscription products configured: timeback_plus_monthly (₹99) & timeback_plus_yearly (₹999) with real-time token validation.
