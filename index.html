Privacy Policy for TimeBack

Google Play Compliant Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Purpose

TimeBack ("we", "our", or "the app") is a digital wellbeing application designed to help users monitor, count, and regulate short-form video consumption (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Snapchat Spotlight).

2. Use of Android AccessibilityService API

TimeBack utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API strictly to:

  • Detect swipe/scroll transitions on vertical short-form video feeds within supported apps.
  • Calculate and update your live reel counter.
  • Enforce your custom daily limit by safely navigating away from the video viewer when your quota is reached.

What TimeBack NEVER does:

  • We DO NOT read, capture, or store private personal messages, chats, or emails.
  • We DO NOT record keystrokes, passwords, or personal credentials.
  • We DO NOT record audio, camera, or video content.
  • We DO NOT transmit your browsing history or account details to external third parties.

3. Local Data Storage & Security

All daily reel counters, limits, and user preferences are stored strictly on your local device using Android encrypted DataStore. No video watching history or account data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.

4. In-App Subscriptions (TimeBack Plus)

All subscription payments are handled securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or process your credit card, debit card, or UPI banking credentials directly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data security, please contact our support team at: support@timeback.app
